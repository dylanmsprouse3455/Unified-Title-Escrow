Dylan Assistant duplicate-safety behavior

Before approval, proposed actions are compared against the current Title Search case, Dylan's open Voice To-Dos, and Dylan's open Call/Callback records.

If an existing or very similar item is found:
- the proposed action is marked as an existing/possible duplicate,
- it is unchecked by default,
- the existing item is shown to Dylan,
- Dylan must explicitly choose Add anyway before that duplicate can be approved.

The server-side approved-action executor also re-checks key exact duplicates immediately before writing, unless the action includes an explicit allow_duplicate approval.
